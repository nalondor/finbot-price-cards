PERPS
What is a perp?
A perpetual future: bet the price of an asset goes up (long) or down (short) with leverage, and no expiry date. You keep the position until you close it or get liquidated. A periodic funding payment between longs and shorts keeps the contract near the real (spot/index) price.
£10 long example
You put up £10 margin and pick 10× leverage → you control about £100 of exposure.
If the asset rises 5%, your position gains ~£5 → roughly +50% on your £10 (before fees/funding).
If it falls 5%, you lose ~£5 (−50% on margin). A big enough drop can liquidate the position and wipe the margin.
UI risk buttons 25% / 50% / 75% = how much of your available balance to use as margin for this trade (not the leverage multiplier).
Leverage & margin
Leverage
Borrowed size vs your margin. Higher = bigger gains and losses.
Margin
Your collateral. Isolated = one position; cross = shared.
Caps: ~20× major crypto · ~10× most equities/RWAs. Collateral is pUSD on Polygon.

Funding & access
Funding: when the perp is rich vs spot, longs usually pay shorts (and vice versa). Anchors the price; protocol takes no cut of funding.
Access: early access often needs a referral code. URL: polymarket.com/perps
Vs Binance / Bybit style
Same core: order book · long/short · leverage · funding · mark price · liquidation.
Different: hybrid DeFi (off-chain match, Polygon settle) · lower max leverage · stocks/commodities too · same balance as prediction markets.
